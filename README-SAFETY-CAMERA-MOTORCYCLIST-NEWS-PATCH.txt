Witney Wire patch: Safety Camera Motorcyclist news article

What this patch does
- Adds a new News article:
  New police motorcycle camera team to target speeding across Thames Valley
- Article ID:
  thames-valley-safety-camera-motorcyclist-team
- Uses the existing Witney Wire traffic.svg road-safety graphic.
- Adds/updates article data copies and Pages Function fallback copies only.

Important image note
Thames Valley Police terms say their site material is copyrighted and should not be copied or reused without explicit permission unless it is specifically stated as available for general use. For that reason, this patch has not copied their photographs into the website. The article credits Thames Valley Police as the source and uses an existing Witney Wire graphic instead.

Files changed
- articles.json and duplicate article-data copies
- functions/article.js and duplicate fallback copies

How to apply
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy the included files and folders into your local local-news-site project folder.
3. Choose replace/overwrite when prompted.
4. Commit in GitHub Desktop.
5. Push origin and let Cloudflare Pages deploy.
