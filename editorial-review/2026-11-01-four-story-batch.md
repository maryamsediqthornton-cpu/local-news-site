## Article 2 — New transport plan sets out 114 actions for Witney and Carterton
Category: Local News / Traffic & Travel · Original announcement: 20 October 2026

Oxfordshire County Council has approved the West Oxfordshire Lowlands Movement and Place Plan, a 25-year transport strategy for Witney, Carterton and surrounding communities.

The plan sets out 15 objectives and 114 supporting actions intended to improve connections between homes, workplaces, schools and local services.

It prioritises walking, wheeling, cycling and public transport while acknowledging the continuing need for cars in rural areas. Priorities include safer walking and cycling networks, better transport hubs and more frequent, reliable bus services.

The council says around 87,000 people live in the area, with approximately 16 per cent commuting to Oxford for work.

The strategy is intended to guide future schemes and support funding bids, but approval does not mean all proposed improvements are funded or have construction dates.

Source: https://news.oxfordshire.gov.uk/plan-to-improve-sustainable-transport-in-west-oxfordshire-is-approved/

## Article 3 — New planning rules change how some West Oxfordshire applications are decided
Category: Local News / Planning · Council announcement: 5 August 2026; proposed implementation 31 October 2026

Changes to the national planning decision process are expected to alter which planning applications are considered by elected councillors and which can be decided by officers.

West Oxfordshire District Council has expressed concerns that fewer decisions would go before councillors, potentially reducing elected members' direct involvement in some local planning decisions.

The government's stated intention is to provide greater consistency and efficiency in the planning system. Residents can continue to comment on applications through the usual consultation processes.

## Article 4 — Police announce additional powers ahead of Oxford United and Reading fixture
Category: Sport / Local News · Thames Valley Police announcement: 30 October 2026

Thames Valley Police has announced a policing operation ahead of Oxford United's home fixture against Reading at the Kassam Stadium on Wednesday 4 November, with kick-off scheduled for 7.45pm.

The force says Section 35 dispersal and Section 60AA powers will apply from 11am on Wednesday until 1am on Thursday 5 November in specified areas of Oxford, Didcot and Reading.

The powers relate to dispersing individuals causing or likely to cause harassment, alarm or distress, and requiring the removal of items used to conceal identity in circumstances permitted by law.

Police have published maps of the designated areas. The measures do not apply throughout Oxfordshire. No general road closure is established by the announcement.

Source: https://www.thamesvalley.police.uk/news/thames-valley/news/2026/october/w.c-26-oct/policing-operation-planned-for-oxford-united-v-reading-fc-fixture/
