Witney Wire news patch – Oxford drug supply sentence

What this patch adds
- A new News article based on the Thames Valley Police article about Craig Ballantyne being jailed for drug supply in Oxford.
- The supplied Thames Valley Police image is included because permission was confirmed by the user.
- The article is set to appear in the News section and on its own article page.
- The article is marked excludeFromHomepage so it does not take over the homepage Top Stories / Latest slots.

Article details
- ID: man-jailed-drug-supply-oxford-craig-ballantyne
- Category: news
- Headline: Oxford man jailed after police witness drug deal in city park
- Image: assets/craig-ballantyne-thames-valley-police.jpeg

Files included
- Article JSON copies
- Core app.js copies needed for homepage exclusion support
- Cloudflare Pages Function article.js copies for article/social preview fallback support
- Image asset

How to apply
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy the included files into your latest local-news-site folder.
3. Replace the existing files when prompted.
4. Commit in GitHub Desktop.
5. Push origin and allow Cloudflare Pages to deploy.
