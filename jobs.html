Bluebell Healthcare Ltd

Domiciliary Care Assistants

A caring career, close to home

Pay
£13.10–£15.77 per hour
Hours
Full-time, part-time & flexible bank shifts
Location
Woodcote, South Oxfordshire and surrounding areas, including Witney, Carterton, Eynsham, Hanborough, Abingdon, Didcot, Wallingford, Wantage, Henley-on-Thames and surrounding villages.

Kind, reliable and looking for rewarding work in your local community? Join Bluebell Healthcare, a friendly, local home care provider based in Woodcote, South Oxfordshire, and help people feel safe, comfortable and supported in their own homes.

The difference you’ll make

Bluebell Healthcare supports people with a range of care needs, including dementia, Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis, stroke recovery, complex care and palliative or end-of-life support.

From personal care and preparing meals to offering companionship, you’ll help people maintain their independence, dignity and everyday routines.

Who they’re looking for

Bluebell Healthcare welcomes experienced Care Assistants, ideally with a driving licence and access to their own vehicle.

New to care? You’re welcome too. Full training is provided for those looking to begin a rewarding career in care.

Why join Bluebell Healthcare?

  • Flexible full-time, part-time and bank opportunities.
  • Competitive hourly pay of £13.10–£15.77.
  • Mileage allowance.
  • Full training and development opportunities.
  • A friendly, supportive local team.
  • Rewarding work making a difference in your community.
