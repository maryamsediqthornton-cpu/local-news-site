Witney Wire Bobbi AI news patch

What this patch adds
- A new News article based on the Thames Valley Police story about Bobbi AI six months after launch.
- The article includes additional local context about the positive impact AI can have in society when used responsibly.
- A new AI-generated illustration is included for the article.
- The article is set to appear in the News section and on the homepage as the newest story.

Article details
- ID: bobbi-ai-community-access-policing
- Headline: Police AI assistant Bobbi helps thousands access support in first six months

Files included
- assets/bobbi-ai-community-policing.png
- articles.json and mirrored article JSON copies
- article.js fallback copies used by the article route
- app.js / assets/app.js / data/app.js for the article image disclaimer

How to apply
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy the included files into your latest local-news-site folder.
3. Replace the existing files when prompted.
4. Commit in GitHub Desktop.
5. Push origin and allow Cloudflare Pages to deploy.
6. Hard refresh the homepage and article page with Ctrl + F5.
